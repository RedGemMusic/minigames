Fish

Getting started

Type !fish in the stream chat to start. You'll unlock the starting area, Animal Island, and immediately cast your line. The game determines whether you catch a fish and what the fish is – no action needed on your part.

You can play every 30 minutes. Catches award currency (G), and new catches are added to your fishing log. Catching new fish unlocks new pools, game worlds, and game series to fish in.

How bites work

When you cast your line, you can get a bite from any fish in the location you're fishing in. At first, you can only fish by series, with all of its fish mixed together. As you catch more unique fish in a series, you'll unlock the ability to focus your efforts on regions (game worlds) in the series, and on pools within each game world. This can help narrow down the odds of getting the fish you're looking for.

How catches work

Whether you catch a fish is determined automatically. Every fish has a catch chance. If you're successful, the fish is caught: you're awarded G and, if it's a new catch, it's added to your fishing log. When you reach certain G or unique catch milestones, you'll unlock new areas and features.

Moving to a new location

When you want to fish somewhere new, you can do it one of two ways:

  1. Use the !move command, e.g. !move Stardew Valley.
    • This can be used even when you're on cooldown, and is usually the safer way to pick a new location.
  2. Use the !fish command and name the location, e.g. !fish Stardew Valley. Be aware, though, that this immediately uses your fishing attempt in the new location.

Whichever you choose, the game finds the best match for the name you entered. If it's ambiguous which area you mean, it asks you to clarify. For example, if you say !fish river, it won't know whether you mean Stardew Valley's river or Animal Island's river.

Provided you've given enough information, it's pretty smart about figuring out where you mean to go, even if you have typos.

Rare spawns

Occasionally, you'll see a notice on the stream that a rare fish is present. Whenever this happens, everyone is allowed a cast to attempt it, independent of their normal fishing cooldown. Just type !fish like usual and you'll attempt to catch the rare fish, even if you don't have the location unlocked.

What's the point?

The fun of the game is whatever you want it to be! You may want to:

  • Complete your fishing log
  • Try to get high ratings
  • Try to catch shiny variants
  • Earn G

Commands

  • !fish [optional location description] – Attempts to catch a fish
  • !fishlog [optional location description] [optional "by" breakdown] – Summarizes your unique catch progress
    • E.g. !fishlog Stardew Valley by pool tells you your progress for each of the pools in Stardew Valley
  • !fishdata – Displays the link to the fish data page
  • !fishhelp – Explains how to start fishing and links to this page
  • !fishseries – Lists the series you've unlocked
  • !fishregions [series] – Lists the regions you've unlocked within that series
  • !fishpools [region] – Lists the pools you've unlocked within that region
  • !move [location description] – Moves you to the location best matching your input
  • !level – Displays your level and progress to the next level
  • !fishrank – Displays your current rank among fishers and what you need to advance to the next rank
  • !fishrankings – Displays the top fishers
  • !showoff [optional fish/location/shiny description] – Displays your best ever catch according to the descriptors you entered
  • !challenge – Displays a reminder of your challenge
  • !setchallenge [easy/medium/hard/elite] – Sets the difficulty you'd like your challenges to be
  • !catchtreasure [on/off] – Turns treasure catching on or off
